2. Data processed by KanMind

When you register or use KanMind, the application processes the information you provide, including your name, email address, and password. Passwords are stored by Django in hashed form and are not stored as plain text.

When you create or use boards, tasks, assignments, and comments, the related content and account references are stored to provide the requested functionality.

3. Login and technical data

After login, the frontend stores an authentication token in the browser session storage so that authenticated API requests can be made during the current browser session. The token is removed when you log out or when the session data is cleared.

The web server may process technical data such as IP address, date and time of access, requested resource, and browser information in server logs. This data is used for secure operation and troubleshooting. According to STRATO's information for website hosting, visitor IP addresses are stored in logfiles for a maximum of seven days to detect and defend against attacks.

4. Purpose and legal basis

Personal data is processed to provide the KanMind service, manage user accounts, and protect the application. The legal basis is the performance of the requested service and, where applicable, legitimate interest in secure and reliable operation.

5. Your rights

Subject to the applicable legal requirements, you may request information about your stored data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, or data portability. You may also object to processing and lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

For requests, contact: vitali.banmann@gmail.com

6. Hosting and third-party resources

This website is hosted by STRATO GmbH. STRATO states that data processing for its hosting services takes place exclusively in Germany, in its data centers in Berlin and Karlsruhe.

Where STRATO processes personal data on our behalf, the applicable agreement on commissioned processing (AVV) under Article 28 GDPR applies. The AVV is available in the STRATO customer login under Your customer data > Commissioned processing or in the contract documents, depending on the contract date. The completed AVV is kept with the project records.

The dashboard loads Chart.js from jsDelivr. When the dashboard is opened, the browser contacts jsDelivr and may transmit technical connection data such as IP address and browser information. The deployed version should replace this external resource with a locally hosted copy if third-party requests are not desired.

7. Retention

Account data, boards, tasks, and comments are retained for as long as the account and the related service are used. They are deleted when the account is deleted, unless a legal retention obligation or the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims requires longer storage.

Server access logs are retained for a maximum of seven days by STRATO according to its hosting information. The retention period for STRATO backups depends on the selected hosting product and backup settings and must be verified in the STRATO customer area before publication.

8. Updates

This privacy policy must be reviewed and updated whenever the application, hosting setup, or data processing changes.
